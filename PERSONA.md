# ProofScope — Persona Test

## Persona

Valeria, 21, Mexico, Business Administration, 7th semester.

She recently completed a project as a Data Analyst for Empresa A. She cleaned data, built a dashboard and presented findings.

She is comfortable with normal websites and AI, but is not technically sophisticated.

## Task

Use ProofScope to see whether project evidence can be used in a different context.

## Journey

Home → Projects → Student Sales Dashboard → Evidence → Try another context → Marketing Analyst + Empresa A → Verify → Result → Human Review.

## Confusions

### Severity 4
She could interpret “Not verified” as meaning she is not skilled.

**Fix:** The result explicitly explains that mismatch does not mean lack of ability. It means the evidence was not authorized for that context.

### Severity 3
She could think ProofScope makes the hiring decision.

**Fix:** Human Review states that a human has final authority.

### Severity 2
She could expect automatic sharing.

**Fix:** The interface states that the student controls sharing.

### Severity 2
She could wonder why the same project cannot transfer automatically.

**Fix:** Evidence is clearly labeled as context-specific.

### Severity 1
She could be unsure what to do after a mismatch.

**Fix:** The result provides Request human review and Try another context.

## Worst confusion fixed

The most important issue was the possibility of interpreting “not verified” as “not capable.”

The product explicitly separates evidence verification from judgment of ability.

## Final question

Why wasn't your evidence verified for the new context?

Expected answer: because the evidence was authorized for the original role and employer context, not because the student lacks the ability.
